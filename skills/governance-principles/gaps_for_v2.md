## Top 5 gaps, ranked by frequency in the April 13 hand version

### Gap 1 · State legislative funding dependency

**What the hand version catches that the Skill cannot.**
- Budget workshop: "$1.18M in restricted HB2 salary already committed — if HB2 changes or sunsets in the next legislative session, the district will have structural salary obligations without the revenue to cover them." → hand emits RED FLAG.
- Bond refunding: "$32.2M hold harmless over 22 years… Hold Harmless was created by HB3 (2019) and modified by HB2 (2023). It is a statutory mechanism, not a constitutional guarantee." → hand emits RED FLAG.
- Combined cross-item: "If the 90th Texas Legislature (2027) restructures school finance, the district could face a bond debt service gap AND an operating budget hole at the same time." → hand emits RED FLAG on the combined exposure.

**Why current taxonomy misses it.** No principle or pattern names "action commits the district to ongoing obligations dependent on a state legislative allotment or formula that can be modified at the next session." RF_15 covers cost-to-taxpayers missing on a single action, not multi-year legislative risk. RF_20 covers balance levels, not funding-stream fragility.

**Proposed addition — new principle.** "Legislative funding is a weather forecast, not a contract." Rule: when the Board adopts or approves an action that depends on a statutory allotment, hold harmless, or formula-driven revenue stream, the Board must explicitly identify the legislative source, confirm that the obligation can be unwound if the source changes, or show a reserve sized to bridge the shortfall.

**Proposed addition — new risk pattern RF_21.** "Obligation dependent on a state legislative funding stream that could change in a future session."
- Fires on: any action that commits district funds to multi-year compensation, debt service coverage, or program operation where the revenue source is an allotment under HB2, HB3, hold harmless, or similar statutory construct.
- Default severity: **RED FLAG** when the obligation runs longer than one legislative session (two years) and the district has no mitigation plan.
- Disqualifier: item includes a stress-test scenario showing what happens if the legislative source is reduced by 25% or 50%, and a Board-approved mitigation (reserve, contingency contract clause, budget trigger).
- Anchors: TEC §11.151(b), TEC §44.002, relevant chapter of the enabling bill (HB3 2019, HB2 2023).

### Gap 2 · Multi-year fund balance decline trajectory

**What the hand version catches that the Skill cannot.**
- Rev/Exp Reports: "Debt Service at 99.92% expended… combined with the Samco estimate of $389,750 I&S Fund Balance for 2025/26, the debt service fund has minimal reserves." → WATCH/RED FLAG.
- Bond refunding: "The I&S Fund Balance declined from $1,943,505 (2022/23) to $794,653 (2024/25) and is estimated at just $389,750 for 2025/26 — an 80% decline in three years." → RED FLAG.
- Budget workshop: "Expenditures are consistently close to or exceeding revenue… the volatility and thin I&S reserves are the most concerning structural issue in the budget." → RED FLAG.

**Why current taxonomy misses it.** RF_20 fires on "account balance at or near zero or negative without prior warning." It catches end-state balances, not the multi-year trajectory that produced them. The hand version's RED FLAG is a trend claim, not a point-in-time claim.

**Proposed addition — broaden RF_20, or add RF_22.** Two options:

*Option A — broaden RF_20 signal.* Add to the "fires on" list: "a fund balance has declined by 25% or more year-over-year, or by 50% or more over three years, regardless of current absolute level."

*Option B — new pattern RF_22.* "Fund balance on multi-year decline trajectory without a corrective plan."
- Default severity: **RED FLAG** when trajectory includes a projected further decline.
- Disqualifier: item includes a dated plan to arrest the decline with milestones.
- Anchors: TEC §44.002, §44.006; FIRST scoring criteria.

Preference: Option B, because the signal and the question pattern ("when does the decline plateau?") are materially different from RF_20's near-zero-balance question ("why did the district not see this coming?").

### Gap 3 · Enrollment decline vs ADA-based funding

**What the hand version catches that the Skill cannot.**
- Budget workshop: "RADA has now declined for TWO consecutive years (−13, then −1). Enrollment is down −3 this year… Since M&O funding is driven by ADA, this directly reduces state revenue." → RED FLAG.
- Same item: "$72K loss of Fast Growth Allotment confirms the state recognizes Brock is no longer a fast-growth district. This is the funding consequence of flat enrollment." → RED FLAG.

**Why current taxonomy misses it.** No pattern addresses demographic trend as a revenue risk. P07 (fiduciary urgency) is close — delay on a structural issue has consequences — but the detection signals are about deferral, not about a trend already in motion.

**Proposed addition — new risk pattern RF_23.** "Revenue base shrinking faster than expense base (enrollment, taxable value, or allotment-driving metric)."
- Fires on: two consecutive years of decline on RADA, ADA, or enrollment; or year-over-year loss of growth-triggered allotments (Fast Growth, Small/Mid-Size); or taxable value growth below 50% of the five-year average.
- Default severity: **WATCH**; escalate to **RED FLAG** when the decline has persisted two or more cycles and the budget being adopted assumes flat or growing revenue.
- Disqualifier: budget already models conservative revenue aligned to the trend, with expense scaling identified.
- Anchors: TEC §42 (school finance), relevant HB provisions, TEC §11.1511.

### Gap 4 · Repeated delegation without multi-year strategy

**What the hand version catches that the Skill cannot.**
- Bond refunding: "The cover sheet says: 'This is the same action we took last year in May…' This tells you something important: the district has been in a cycle of annual parameter order authorizations. A healthy debt structure does not require annual refunding authorizations. This annual cycle should prompt the board to ask for a multi-year debt management strategy." → hand emits WATCH framed as a structural question.

**Why current taxonomy misses it.** RF_02 catches delegation without oversight on a single action. It doesn't catch the same delegation being requested year after year without a presented multi-year plan.

**Proposed addition — new risk pattern RF_24.** "Board asked to authorize the same delegation annually without a multi-year strategy presented."
- Fires on: agenda item cover sheet or minutes history indicates this is a recurring annual authorization of the same delegated authority (pricing officer, contracting authority, spending authority) in two or more consecutive years.
- Default severity: **WATCH**; escalate to **RED FLAG** if the repeated delegation involves debt or capital spending above 1% of annual budget.
- Disqualifier: item includes a five-year strategy or plan with milestones that justifies the recurring authorization.
- Anchors: TGC §1371.053, TEC §45.001, local policy on delegation.

### Gap 5 · New material commitments voted without prior discussion

**What the hand version catches that the Skill cannot.**
- TIA Payouts: "Prior Discussion Date: None… Should this be pulled from any consent grouping for fuller discussion, given it commits the district to a new state program?" → hand emits a question that is effectively a WATCH.

**Why current taxonomy misses it.** P05 (discussion is not action) covers the reverse case — deferring a discussion item. Nothing catches the case where a first-time material commitment is placed on an action agenda without any prior discussion. P11 (specificity on the record) is close if the item is vaguely described, but does not fire on a clearly-described first-time commitment.

**Proposed addition — new risk pattern RF_25, or extension to P05.** "First-time material commitment placed on action agenda without prior board discussion."
- Fires on: cover sheet lists "Prior Discussion Date: None" AND the item commits the district to entering a new state program, signing a multi-year contract, adopting a new policy framework, or delegating new authority.
- Default severity: **WATCH**; escalate to **RED FLAG** when the commitment is a new ongoing financial obligation above a materiality threshold.
- Disqualifier: item is ministerial (routine grant acceptance under a previously-approved framework, renewal of an existing program).
- Anchors: BE(LOCAL) agenda-posting clauses, TEC §11.151(b).
