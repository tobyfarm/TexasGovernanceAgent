The governance-authority backbone. Every board-power and board-duty argument

**Title.** Powers and Duties of Board of Trustees of Independent School District — subsection (b), exclusive governance authority.

### TEC §11.1512

**Title.** Specific Powers and Duties of Superintendent.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TEC §11.1512 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.11.htm#11.1512 -->

**Cross-references:** §11.201 (duties of superintendent); §11.151 (exclusive board authority).

### TEC §11.201(d)

**Title.** Duties of Superintendent — subsection (d), day-to-day management.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TEC §11.201(d) goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.11.htm#11.201 -->

**Cross-references:** §11.151(b) (board governance authority); §11.1512 (superintendent's specific duties).
