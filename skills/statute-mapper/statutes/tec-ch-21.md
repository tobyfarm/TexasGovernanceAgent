# Texas Education Code — Chapter 21 (Educators)

Teacher and educator personnel code. Covers certification, contract types
(probationary, continuing, term), renewal/non-renewal deadlines, and the
Teacher Incentive Allotment (TIA). Heavily cited in the Brock April 13,
2026 packet for the teacher-contract consent item and the TIA authorization.

---

### TEC §21.003

**Title.** Certification Required.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TEC §21.003 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.21.htm#21.003 -->

**Common usage in board context:** Cited on new-hire teacher consents and alternative certification questions. Alternative certification pathways produce the same standard teaching certificate as traditional programs.

---

### TEC §21.102

**Title.** Probationary Contracts — term and limitations.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TEC §21.102 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.21.htm#21.102 -->

**Cross-references:** §21.103 (probationary termination); §21.206 (renewal/non-renewal deadline).

**Common usage:** Cited on probationary-contract approvals. Maximum 1-year term, renewable for up to 2 additional years (3 years total). Teachers with 5+ years of prior experience in the last 8 years may serve only 1 year of probation.

---

### TEC §21.103

**Title.** Probationary Contract Termination.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TEC §21.103 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.21.htm#21.103 -->

**Common usage:** Cited on mid-year or end-of-contract probationary terminations. Notice must be delivered no later than the 10th day before the last day of instruction.

---

### TEC §21.206

**Title.** CRITICAL DEADLINE — Notice of Proposed Nonrenewal (term contracts).

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TEC §21.206 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.21.htm#21.206 -->

**Common usage:** The hard deadline: not later than the 10th day before the last day of instruction, the board must notify each teacher whose contract is about to expire whether the board proposes renewal or non-renewal. **Failure to give notice = automatic renewal.** Missed deadlines commit the district to another year of employment regardless of intent. Pairs with Principle 7 (fiduciary urgency).

---

### TEC §21.3521

**Title.** Local Optional Teacher Designation System — Teacher Incentive Allotment (TIA).

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TEC §21.3521 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.21.htm#21.3521 -->

**Common usage:** Cited on TIA authorization items. Districts may designate teachers as Recognized, Exemplary, or Master. TEA verifies and issues allotments. Districts must spend at least 90% of the allotment on teacher compensation at the campus where the teacher works.
