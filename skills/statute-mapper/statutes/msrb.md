# MSRB Rules (Municipal Securities Rulemaking Board)

Federal rules governing conduct of municipal securities dealers and municipal
advisors. Texas school districts are "municipal entity clients" under MSRB
rules, which means the district's **governing body** (the Board) is the
fiduciary beneficiary of an advisor's duty — not administration.

Pairs with `tec-ch-45.md` (bond authority) and Principle 16 (role clarity).

---

### MSRB G-17

**Title.** Conduct of Municipal Securities and Municipal Advisory Activities — fair dealing.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of MSRB Rule G-17 goes here as a blockquote. -->

**Common usage in board context:** The baseline standard of fair dealing that applies to every underwriter, dealer, and advisor interacting with the district. Narrower than G-42 (no fiduciary duty), but still prohibits unfair practices and material misrepresentation.

### MSRB G-42

**Title.** Duties of Non-Solicitor Municipal Advisors — fiduciary duty of loyalty and care to municipal entity clients.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of MSRB Rule G-42 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://www.msrb.org/rules/rule-g-42 -->

**Cross-references:** MSRB G-17 (fair dealing); SEC Rule 15Ba1 (municipal advisor registration).

**Common usage in board context:** Cited whenever a municipal advisor deflects, routes through administration, or declines to speak directly with trustees. The fiduciary duty runs to the **governing body** (the Board), not to the Superintendent or CFO. Pairs with Risk Pattern #8 (advisors refusing to speak directly with trustees) and the SAMCO Q&A line of questioning.
