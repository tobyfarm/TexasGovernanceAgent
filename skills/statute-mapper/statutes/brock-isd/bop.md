# BOP — Board Operating Procedures

Brock ISD's Board Operating Procedures. A procedural companion to
board-adopted policy — **not** a replacement for or override of policy.
Subordinate per principles.md §I Principle 1.

When BOP contradicts or narrows BE(LOCAL), the adopted policy controls.
BOP can be amended by Board action outside the policy-amendment process;
policy can only be amended through adoption in open session. That
asymmetry is what makes BOP subordinate.
