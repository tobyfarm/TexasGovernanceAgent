# SEC Rules (Securities and Exchange Commission)

Federal securities-law rules that apply to municipal issuers. Narrow but
load-bearing — non-compliance is a securities-law violation.

Pairs with `msrb.md` (broker-dealer and advisor rules) and `tec-ch-45.md`
(district bond authority).

### SEC Rule 15c2-12

**Title.** Municipal Securities Disclosure — continuing disclosure requirements.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of SEC Rule 15c2-12 goes here as a blockquote. -->

**Cross-references:** MSRB EMMA filing system; MSRB Rule G-32 (primary-offering disclosure).

**Common usage in board context:** The "continuing disclosure" rule. Requires the district to provide annual financial reports to the MSRB within a defined window (typically 6 months after fiscal year end) plus event notices within 10 business days of triggering events (rating changes, defaults, amendments, etc.). Cited in the parameter-order language for bond issuances. **Non-compliance is a securities law violation** — a failure to file is a real enforcement risk, not a clerical issue.
