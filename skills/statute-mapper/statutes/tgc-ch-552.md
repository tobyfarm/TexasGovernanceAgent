# Texas Government Code — Chapter 552 (Texas Public Information Act)

Transparency's statutory backbone on the records side. Pairs with TGC Ch. 551
(TOMA) on the meetings side. A trustee who can't get answers through normal
channels can file a PIA request — it's a legitimate tool, not adversarial
(see Principle 14).

---

### TGC §552.001

**Title.** Policy; Construction.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TGC §552.001 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.552.htm#552.001 -->

**Common usage:** The liberal-construction rule — the Act is to be liberally construed in favor of granting requests for information. Cited when a district tries to narrow disclosure.

---

### TGC §552.002

**Title.** Definition of Public Information.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TGC §552.002 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.552.htm#552.002 -->

**Common usage:** The scope-of-PIA threshold. "Public information" is broader than most administrators assume — it covers information held by a governmental body or a third party on its behalf.

### TGC §552.021

**Title.** Availability of Public Information.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TGC §552.021 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.552.htm#552.021 -->

**Common usage:** The presumption of availability. Public information shall be available to the public during normal business hours.
