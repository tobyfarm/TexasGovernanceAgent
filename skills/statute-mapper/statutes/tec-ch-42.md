# Texas Education Code — Chapter 42 (Foundation School Program / School Finance)

The state school-finance framework. Average Daily Attendance (ADA) is the
primary driver of Maintenance & Operations (M&O) funding. Cited by the
risk-flagger (RF_23) when an enrollment or ADA decline threatens the
district revenue base.

Pairs with `tx-session-laws.md` (HB3 2019, HB2 2023) because the current
structure of Chapter 42 is the product of those sessions. Hold Harmless
and HB2 Teacher Retention Allotment mechanisms are statutory constructs
that the legislature can modify at the next session (see Agent C's
gaps_for_v2.md Gap 1 on state legislative funding dependency).
