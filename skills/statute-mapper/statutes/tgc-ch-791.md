# Texas Government Code — Chapter 791 (Interlocal Cooperation Act)

Authorizes local governments (including school districts) to contract with
each other for governmental functions or services. Used in the Brock
April 13, 2026 packet for the Region 11 ESC interlocal cooperation
agreement.

Pairs with TEC Chapter 8 (which creates the Regional Education Service
Centers) and TEC §44.031 (which exempts interlocal cooperative
purchasing from individual bid requirements).
