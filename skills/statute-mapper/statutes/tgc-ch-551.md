# Texas Government Code — Chapter 551 (Open Meetings Act / TOMA)

Transparency's statutory backbone. Every posting, closed-session, final-action,
and certified-agenda question traces back to this chapter.

The general rule (§551.002): every regular, special, or called meeting of a
governmental body shall be open to the public, except as provided by this
chapter. Closed-session exceptions are narrow and must be specifically stated
before entering.

### TGC §551.041

**Title.** Notice of Meeting Required.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TGC §551.041 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.551.htm#551.041 -->

**Cross-references:** §551.043 (72-hour timing and accessibility); §551.101 (announcement before closed session).

### TGC §551.074

**Title.** Personnel Matters; Closed Meeting.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TGC §551.074 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.551.htm#551.074 -->

**Cross-references:** §551.101 (must announce before entering); §551.102 (no final action in closed session).

**Common usage:** Covers deliberations about **specific individuals** — appointment, employment, evaluation, discipline, complaint. Does **not** cover general personnel policy, compensation structures, or district-wide staffing decisions. Pairs with Risk Pattern #18 (deliberation that belongs in open session routed to closed).

### TGC §551.101

**Title.** Requirement to First Convene in Open Meeting; announcement of specific section before entering closed session.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TGC §551.101 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.551.htm#551.101 -->

**Common usage:** The presiding officer must announce **which specific section** authorizes the closed session before entering.
