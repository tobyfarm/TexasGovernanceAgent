# Texas Education Code — Chapter 45 (School District Funds and Bonds)

Where bond issuance authority lives. Every bond sale, refunding, and
restructuring presented to the board traces back to this chapter. Pairs
with `pricing-officer.md` for delegation questions and `msrb.md` for
municipal-advisor fiduciary duty.

---

### TEC §45.004

**Title.** Refunding Bonds — district authority to issue.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TEC §45.004 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.45.htm#45.004 -->

**Cross-references:** TGC Ch. 1207 (statewide refunding framework); TGC Ch. 1371 (general public-security authority); TEC §45.001 (primary bond authority).

**Common usage in board context:** Primary authority for refunding bonds. Refunding bonds do not require a voter election and must be submitted to the Texas Attorney General for examination. The board may sell at public or private sale. Pairs with Risk Pattern #3 — always distinguish real savings from term extension.

### TEC §45.051

**Title.** Permanent School Fund Bond Guarantee Program — authorization.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TEC §45.051 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.45.htm#45.051 -->

**Cross-references:** Tex. Const. art. VII §5 (PSF constitutional basis); TEC §45.061 (withholding on default).

**Common usage in board context:** First section of Subchapter C of Chapter 45, which governs the PSF Bond Guarantee Program. PSF backing provides AAA-level credit rating and lower interest rates. Cited when the parameter order invokes PSF guarantee.

---

### TEC §45.061

**Title.** PSF Bond Guarantee — Comptroller Withholding on Default.

<!-- TODO: verbatim text of TEC §45.061 goes here as a blockquote. -->
<!-- Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.45.htm#45.061 -->

**Common usage:** The enforcement hook. If the district defaults on a PSF-guaranteed bond, the Comptroller is authorized to withhold state funds from the district to cover debt service. Cited as the reason PSF backing imposes real fiscal discipline.
