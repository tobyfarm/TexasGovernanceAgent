# Texas Health and Safety Code — selected provisions

Environmental and public-health statutes relevant to school-district
operations. Most commonly cited in transportation procurement contexts
through the Texas Emissions Reduction Plan (TERP) and the Texas Clean
School Bus Program.

---

### HSC Chapter 390

**Title.** Texas Emissions Reduction Plan — Texas Clean School Bus Program.

**Cross-references:** TEC §44.031 (competitive bidding); TEC §44.031(a)(5) (school bus purchase threshold).

**Common usage in board context:** The statutory framework for TERP grants that defray the cost of lower-emission school buses (propane, electric). Cited on bus-purchase items where a TERP grant is reducing district cost — pairs with the PF_A POSITIVE flag for grant-backed procurement. The April 13, 2026 Brock packet's Item 11 references a TERP grant covering 63% of bus cost.
